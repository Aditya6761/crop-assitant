# Reflection

_Fill this in yourself — the grader is explicitly looking for honest
thinking, not "it was great!" A generic reflection is a Revise, not a Pass.
The prompts below are here to structure your answer, not to be copied._

## What was hardest, and why?

_e.g. Was it getting the model to reliably return valid JSON? Handling the
edge case where the user marks soil type "not sure"? Writing tests for the
async fetch flow? Say what actually gave you trouble and why it was hard —
not just what the task was._

## What would you do differently next time?

_e.g. A design decision you'd revisit, a piece of scope you'd cut or add,
a testing approach you'd start with instead of bolting on later._

## One thing you learned that surprised you

_e.g. Something about prompting for structured output, an accessibility
issue you didn't expect, a deployment quirk. Pick one specific, concrete
moment rather than a general takeaway._
